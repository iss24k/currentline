# Arizona route and aerial imagery

The flight visits the Eleven Mile Solar Center in Pinal County and Meta’s data center campus in Mesa, Arizona. Salt River Project (SRP), Meta and Ørsted announced a contract under which Meta would receive the majority of the solar and storage project’s output. The two sites participate in the shared SRP grid. The dotted aerial connection is a geographic flight between the sites. On the shared grid, electrical power flows through the interconnected network according to its electrical state and demand. The animation does not assign the plant’s electrons or its power to one conductor through that network. [SRP and Ørsted announcement](https://media.srpnet.com/meta-teams-up-with-srp-and-rsted-to-support-data-center-with-clean-energy-from-new-solar-and-battery--project/)

## Imagery credit and processing

All bundled photos come from the USDA National Agriculture Imagery Program (NAIP). USDA identifies its acquired aerial photography and orthophotography as public domain. These are orthorectified aerial photographs with a satellite-style camera presentation. The project attributes USDA NAIP in the atlas. [USDA program and public-domain designation](https://catalog.data.gov/dataset/national-agriculture-imagery-program-naip-imagery) · [USDA aerial imagery terms](https://www.fpacbc.usda.gov/geospatial-services/customer-services)

- `arizona.webp` uses the Arizona State Land Department's 2023 Arizona NAIP mosaic. The regional export is 6,144 × 4,096 pixels and shows the terrain between the two sites. [Arizona imagery catalog](https://azgeo-open-data-agic.hub.arcgis.com/datasets/dedabb5657644beea8769175d9e56889/about) · [AZGeo imagery export service](https://azgeo.az.gov/arcgis/rest/services/imagery/NAIP2023/ImageServer)
- `eleven-mile*.webp` and `mesa*.webp` use 2025 USDA NAIP natural-color mosaics from the original 60 cm imagery, with 4,800 × 3,200 pixel exports. The solar mosaics use June 7 and August 2, 2025 acquisitions (August 2 at the main solar array detail). The Mesa mosaics use June 7, 2025 acquisitions. At the overview-to-detail transition, patches may show construction completed since the regional 2023 capture. [USDA acquisition updates](https://naip-usdaonline.hub.arcgis.com/) · [Planetary Computer NAIP catalog](https://planetarycomputer.microsoft.com/dataset/naip)
- `arizona-relief.png` uses the U.S. Geological Survey’s public-domain 3D Elevation Program bare-earth model, sampled onto the same extent as the regional photograph at 768 × 512 pixels. Red and green encode elevation to 0.04 m steps for the renderer. Sampling resolution governs the true terrain detail. [USGS 3DEP](https://www.usgs.gov/3d-elevation-program) · [Elevation raster service](https://elevation.nationalmap.gov/arcgis/rest/services/3DEPElevation/ImageServer)
- `cover.webp` is a lightweight preview derived from the same 2023 regional image.
- All aerial photos are baked locally in Web Mercator (EPSG:3857) and packaged as WebP. The raster extents are recorded in `src/atlas-data.js`. Ground distances use latitude and longitude because Web Mercator scale increases with latitude. The app can display the imagery offline.

The equipment diagrams teach representative electrical configurations. Public sources confirm the power project, grid connections and campus location. The interior models are educational examples.
